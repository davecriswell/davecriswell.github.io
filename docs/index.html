How I work

  • Systems first, then the tool

    Understand how the work actually flows and who owns what, then choose software that fits. Not the other way around.

  • Meet people where they already work

    Put the form in the app coaches already open. Adoption is the real test of a system, and the hardest one to pass.

  • Show the math

    If a number can’t be traced back to its source, it isn’t ready to make decisions with.

  • Start small, then scale

    Ship a manual fallback now, pilot with one team or region, learn what breaks, and earn the right to roll it out.

  • Be honest about status

    “Automated through step four, manual after that” is more useful than “fully automated.”

  • Write it down

    A process that lives in one person’s head isn’t a system yet.
