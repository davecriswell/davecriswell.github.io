About

I’m a native New Englander and a calm presence by nature. The part of my work I care about most is the people on the other end of a system: the coach filling out a form after a long weekend of games, the parent registering a kid for the first time, the director who needs one number they can trust.

What I’ve always enjoyed most is watching how people actually use something, where they get stuck and what they skip, and adjusting from there. That’s what drew me to software, and it’s still how I approach every system I build.

I coached high school and club soccer for about a decade, and it turned out to be similar work. You set people up to succeed, watch what actually happens, and adjust, patiently and without drama.

I’m also interested in where AI is genuinely useful in operations, and where it’s just noise. This site is part of that: I built it with Claude Code as a way to learn.
