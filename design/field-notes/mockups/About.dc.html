About

A New Englander with a calm temperament, who thinks first about the people on the other end of a system.

I like watching how people use something: where they get stuck, what they skip. Then adjusting. That's what drew me to software, and it still guides how I work.

I coached high school and club soccer for about ten years. It's similar work: set people up to succeed, watch, adjust with patience.

These days I'm interested in where AI is genuinely useful in operations and where it's hype. I built this site with Claude Code to learn by doing.
